How to play

Shapes march down the lane toward you. Stop them before they reach the bottom. You have three attack styles, each with its own bar:

  • Strength (red): swipe through enemies to slice them, press and hold to charge a Slam, tap a side lane to place a Blade turret.
  • Agility (yellow): pull down and let go to shoot an arrow, press and hold to throw a Fire Bomb, tap a side lane to place a Pull Turret.
  • Magic (blue): swipe up to fire an Arcane Missile, press and hold to drop an Ice Field, tap a side lane to place a Rune turret.

Switch attack style by tapping either side of the bar, or swiping across it. When a bar is full, hold it until its frame turns gold, then let go to fire that style's ultimate. The speed button changes the battle speed (x1, x2, x4).

Common questions

Why am I not earning XP?

At normal speed, you only earn XP on waves at or above your level. Once your level is ahead of the wave, speed the battle up to x2 or x4 to keep earning XP, or keep playing until the waves catch up.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to another device?

Your progress is saved only on your own device. There are no accounts, so it can't be moved or synced to another device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I start over?

Go to Options and choose Reset. This erases all of your progress and can't be undone.

How do I turn off the music, sound or vibration?

In Options you can switch the music, the sound effects and the haptic feedback on or off.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Polywars is fully offline.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later.
