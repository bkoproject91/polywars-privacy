Polywars

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Polywars ("the game") is developed and published by Tamas Barko (BKO Project 91), "we" in this policy. This policy explains what information the game handles and how.

The short version: Polywars does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Polywars has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game has no online features and sends no data from your device to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the game keeps a small save on your own device only. It contains:

  • your level, experience and skill points
  • the skills you have unlocked
  • the artifacts you have unlocked and equipped, and the builds you have discovered
  • your in-game statistics, such as waves reached and enemies defeated
  • which tutorials you have already seen
  • your settings, such as sound, music and haptics

This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time with Options → Reset in the game, or by deleting the app. In the web version of the game, the same save is kept in your browser's local storage instead, and clearing your browser's site data for the game erases it.

Third-party services

  • No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking. The game does not include any such services.
  • The game engine. Polywars is built with the Unity game engine. Unity's analytics and crash-reporting features are switched off in the game.
  • Apple. If you download Polywars from the App Store, or test it through TestFlight, Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy and your device settings, such as purchase records, and crash reports if you have chosen to share them with developers. We do not control that information. Apple's privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children's privacy

Polywars does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live). It can be played without sharing any personal information.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold any of your personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. All of your game data is on your device and under your control.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future update adds an online feature, we will update this page and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.
